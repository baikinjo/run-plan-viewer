    Cycle 3 · Fresh Start

    Effort guide
    Easy / recovery
    RPE 2-3 and full-sentence talk test. HR cap 145 in Weeks 1-2, then 150.
    Strides
    About 20 seconds relaxed and controlled, never sprinting, with full walk/jog recovery.
    Threshold
    RPE 6-7 and sustainable form. HR ceiling 175; ease pace if HR stays above it.
    Intervals
    RPE 7-8, repeatable across every repetition, never all-out.
    Time trial
    All-out only after the Week 5 readiness gate passes.
    Before-run and stop rules

    Before: ordinary daily activity should feel normal and the easy warmup must pass the full-sentence talk test. If warmup is RPE 4+ or HR is about 10 bpm above the recent easy pattern, replace quality with 30 minutes easy or stop.

    Stop: chest pain or tightness, palpitations, dizziness, faintness, disproportionate breathlessness, fever or systemic symptoms, sharp or focal pain, altered gait, nausea, confusion, or escalating heat distress.

    Weekly adjustment rules
    • Never make up a missed run, add missed distance elsewhere, or stack quality sessions.
    • Preserve sessions in this order: Monday quality, Saturday long, Wednesday easy/strides, Sunday recovery.
    • If fewer than three runs are completed, repeat the next week's dose and shift the remaining cycle and TT by seven days.
    • If Thursday lifting causes soreness of 4/10 or more, cap Saturday at the prior completed long-run duration.
    • If soreness changes gait, skip the run.
    Oct 10 time-trial gate
    1. No fever, chest symptoms, unusual breathlessness, palpitations, or illness recurrence.
    2. At least three runs completed in each of Weeks 3-5.
    3. Week 5 threshold stayed at RPE 7 or lower without sustained HR above 175.
    4. Week 5 long run reached at least 70 minutes without altered gait, focal pain, or abnormal next-day fatigue.
    5. Easy running returned to stable conversational effort without unexplained HR/RPE rise across two consecutive runs.

    If any condition fails, replace the TT with 45 minutes easy and extend the rebuild.
